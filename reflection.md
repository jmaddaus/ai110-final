# Reflection: Profile Comparisons

## Happy Pop Fan vs Chill Lofi Listener

The pop fan's top results were mostly high-energy tracks with positive vibes (Sunrise City, Gym Hero), while the lofi listener got low-energy, mellow tracks (Midnight Coding, Library Rain). This makes sense because the two profiles are almost opposite in energy (0.8 vs 0.4) and mood (happy vs chill). The one overlap was that both lists pulled from a mix of genres beyond their favorite, since there are only so many pop or lofi songs in the catalog.

## Intense Rock Fan vs Relaxed Acoustic

These two profiles are on completely different ends of the energy spectrum (0.9 vs 0.3). The rock fan got Storm Runner and Gym Hero at the top, both high-energy and intense. The acoustic listener got Campfire Songs and Coffee Shop Stories, both low-energy and relaxed. What stands out is that the acoustic profile got a nearly perfect score (4.0) for Campfire Songs because it matched on genre, mood, and energy all at once. The rock fan's top pick (Storm Runner, 3.99) was close but not quite perfect because its energy is 0.91 instead of exactly 0.9.

## Happy Pop Fan vs Intense Rock Fan

Both of these profiles want high energy, but different genres and moods. Gym Hero actually shows up in both lists, which is interesting. For the pop fan it ranks #2 (genre match), and for the rock fan it ranks #2 (mood match). That shows how a single song can score well for different reasons depending on who is asking. The pop fan's list leans more positive and upbeat, while the rock fan's list is darker and heavier, which lines up with what you would expect.

## Chill Lofi Listener vs Relaxed Acoustic

These are the two "calm" profiles. Both want low energy and a laid-back mood. The main difference is genre preference (lofi vs folk). Their top results do not overlap at all, which shows that the genre weight (2.0 points) is strong enough to push them into different parts of the catalog even when their energy and mood preferences are similar. If genre mattered less, these two profiles would probably share more recommendations.
